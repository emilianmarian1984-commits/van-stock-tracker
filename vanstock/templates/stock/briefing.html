60-second van security briefing

Read once. Takes a minute. You'll only see this on your first login.

  1. Lock your phone. PIN or fingerprint. This app stays logged in for 30 minutes - an unlocked phone left on the dashboard is an open stock list.
  2. Three random words. Your password is yours only. Not your email one, not written on the sun visor.
  3. Log out on borrowed devices. Customer's laptop, mate's tablet - always tap Log out.
  4. Ignore login links in texts. Open the app from your own bookmark. No supplier needs your password.
  5. Check History. If stock moved and it wasn't you, change your password straight away.
